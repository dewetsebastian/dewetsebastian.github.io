Intro

I was born and raised in Stellenbosch, South Africa. Having had an interest in Computer Science from a very young age, I decided to follow this career path. My passion for the German language motivated me to move to Germany, where I am almost done with my Masters degree. I am interested in full-stack development, and through my experience I have found a liking to Java Spring Boot development.

Aside from Computer Science, my interests include Business, Language and Psychology. I am fluent in German, English and Afrikaans. In the future, I plan on learning Danish and Dutch.

Experience

In terms of work experience, I am currently working as a working student at Rewe Digital GmbH on a 20-hour basis, where I do full-stack development with Angular in the frontend and Spring Boot in the backend. Previously, I worked as a full-stack software engineer at Matogen in 2023 in order to gain some practical skills before starting my Masters. The technologies I worked with were Angular, Python, Git and various AWS tools. I learnt a tremendous amount in this year, ranging from the aforementioned technical skills to soft skills. I managed to learn a lot of technologies at a rapid pace. I also learnt how to communicate effectively and handle pressure. Furthermore, I interned at Praelexis in the June/July holidays in 2022. There I mainly worked with Python. Lastly, in the December holidays of 2019, I worked at a wine farm. This was particularly useful in strengthening my soft skills. This experience taught me how to work with people.

In terms of University related projects, I have done the following:
In Computer Science 344 we worked with a company to develop software. My group was tasked to create a machine learning application for Impact.com. This helped me to learn about the software development process.

In Computer Science 334 we created two full-stack web applications. With these projects, I learnt a lot about practical Computer Science topics.

In Computer Science 214 (Algorithms and Data Structures) we made software that has the ability to count spots on a Cheetah using Java. This was a really cool project and I learnt a lot about writing efficient code in this course.

In Computer Science 244, we built our own compiler in C. This helped me to understand programming languages on a deeper level.

In first year Computer Science we built a variety of games using Java and Git. This helped me to understand how to develop software using tools such as Git, scripting and so forth.

Most of my GitHub projects are private. I will gladly show them on request.
